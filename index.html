Touch controls

  • Tabs under the waveforms switch between Deck A, the Mixer, Deck B and the Library. The bar at the bottom always has play, cue, sync and the crossfader.
  • Knobs: slide your finger up or down. Double-tap to reset.
  • Faders: drag the handle or tap the track. Double-tap to reset.
  • Jog wheel: in VINYL mode, touch it to scratch. With VINYL off, spin it to nudge the tempo.
  • Waveforms: drag the big waveform to scratch. Tap the overview to jump.
  • Hot cues: tap to set or jump. Press and hold a set pad to delete it.
  • Multi-touch: use two fingers at once, e.g. one on the crossfader and one on a fader.
  • On iPhone, turn up the media volume. If there's still no sound, check that Silent Mode is off.

Keyboard

Mouse

  • Knobs: drag up/down or scroll. Double-click resets. Hold Shift for fine control.
  • Faders: drag, click the track or scroll. Double-click resets.
  • Jog wheel: in VINYL mode grab it to scratch. With VINYL off, spin it to speed up or slow down a playing track (nudge). Scroll over it to nudge.
  • Big waveforms: drag to scratch or scrub, scroll to zoom.
  • Small overview: click to jump anywhere in the track.
  • Hot cue pads: click to set or jump. Right-click or Shift-click deletes.

Your first mix

  1. Tap ▶ for deck A in the bottom bar. Keep the crossfader in the middle.
  2. In the Mixer tab, pull deck B's volume fader all the way down, then tap ▶ for B.
  3. Tap B's SYNC, or practise by ear with the pitch fader on the Deck B tab until the kicks line up.
  4. Start B on the first beat of a phrase (the bright bar lines on the waveform). Use hot cues to restart it in the right place.
  5. In the Mixer, tap B's LOW K (kill) and raise B's volume fader.
  6. On a phrase change, swap the bass: kill A's low and un-kill B's. Then slide the crossfader to B.
  1. Press F to play deck A. Keep the crossfader in the middle.
  2. Pull deck B's volume all the way down (hold H), then press J to start it.
  3. Match B's tempo to A: press L (SYNC), or practise by ear with N/M and nudge with ,/. until the kicks line up.
  4. Start B on the first beat of a phrase (the bright bar lines on the waveform). Use hot cues to restart it in the right place.
  5. Kill B's bass (Shift+U) and raise its volume (hold Y).
  6. On a phrase change, swap the bass: kill A's low (Shift+Q) and bring B's back. Then fade A out (hold G).

Tip: with Split Cue on and headphones in, the 🎧 channels play in your left ear and the master in your right, like a real DJ booth.
