DJ Practice Lab

Two decks, a mixer, effects and a crate of copyright-free tracks. Practise beatmatching, EQ blends, loops and scratching with your keyboard and mouse.

Headphones recommended. Press ? at any time to see every key.

Keyboard

Mouse

  • Knobs: drag up/down or scroll. Double-click resets. Hold Shift for fine control.
  • Faders: drag, click the track or scroll. Double-click resets.
  • Jog wheel: in VINYL mode grab it to scratch. With VINYL off, spin it to speed up or slow down a playing track (nudge). Scroll over it to nudge.
  • Big waveforms: drag to scratch or scrub, scroll to zoom.
  • Small overview: click to jump anywhere in the track.
  • Hot cue pads: click to set or jump. Right-click or Shift-click deletes.

Your first mix

  1. Press F to play deck A. Keep the crossfader in the middle.
  2. Pull deck B's volume all the way down (hold H), then press J to start it.
  3. Match B's tempo to A: press L (SYNC), or practise by ear with N/M and nudge with ,/. until the kicks line up.
  4. Start B on the first beat of a phrase (the bright bar lines on the waveform). Use hot cues to restart it in the right place.
  5. Kill B's bass (Shift+U) and raise its volume (hold Y).
  6. On a phrase change, swap the bass: kill A's low (Shift+Q) and bring B's back. Then fade A out (hold G).

Tip: with Split Cue on and headphones in, the 🎧 channels play in your left ear and the master in your right, like a real DJ booth.
